Questions owners ask

I already have a Google listing and Justdial. Why a website?

Your listing shows you exist; your website shows why someone should choose you — services, timings, photos and an easy WhatsApp button. Google also shows a “Website” button on your profile.

Do I pay before seeing anything?

No. I build a free demo with your name and services first. You pay 50% only if you want to go ahead.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and email.

What do I need to give you?

Your services, timings, WhatsApp number, 8–15 photos and your logo if you have one. A 15-minute chat is enough.

Are there monthly charges?

Hosting is free. Domain renewal is roughly ₹800–₹1,000 a year. The care plan is optional.
